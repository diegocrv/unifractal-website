Cuándo un proveedor puede recibir datos de salud

Sólo enviamos datos de salud a un modelo de lenguaje cuyo proveedor cumple las cuatro condiciones:

  1. no entrena modelos con lo que recibe;
  2. no conserva el contenido más allá de la solicitud, salvo para prevenir abusos o por obligación legal;
  3. está nombrado en el Aviso de Privacidad y en esta página;
  4. lo contratamos directamente o, si hay un intermediario, con el destino fijado de antemano.

This page accompanies the Privacy Policy. It lists the providers whose language models generate the assistant's replies, what each one does with what we send them, and when we verified it. When a provider is added, removed, or changes its policy, this page is updated, with the date.

When a provider may receive health data

We only send health data to a language model whose provider meets all four conditions:

  1. it does not train models on what it receives;
  2. it does not retain the content beyond the request, except to prevent abuse or when required by law;
  3. it is named in the Privacy Policy and on this page;
  4. we contract with it directly or, if there is an intermediary, with the destination fixed in advance.
